# Northstar Shop FAQ

Northstar Shop is a fictional online retailer used by this project.

## Orders

### How can I track my order?

Open **Account > Orders**, select the order and choose **Track shipment**. A tracking link is also included in the shipping confirmation email. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement after dispatch.

### Can I change or cancel an order?

An order can be changed or cancelled from **Account > Orders** while its status is **Processing**. Once the status is **Packing** or **Shipped**, it cannot be changed or cancelled. An unwanted eligible item can be returned after delivery.

### What if an item is missing from my order?

Check whether the order was split into multiple shipments in **Account > Orders**. If every shipment is marked delivered and an item is still missing, call support at +1-800-555-0147.

## Shipping

### How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates exclude weekends and public holidays.

### How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free for orders of $50 or more and costs $5.99 for smaller orders. Express shipping costs $14.99. The checkout page shows the final shipping charge before payment.

### Do you ship internationally?

Northstar Shop currently ships only within the contiguous United States. It does not ship to international addresses, PO boxes, Alaska or Hawaii.

### My tracking says delivered, but I cannot find the package. What should I do?

Check entrances, mailrooms and safe places around the delivery address. Ask household members or building staff whether they accepted it. Carriers sometimes mark packages delivered several hours early. If the package is still missing after 24 hours, call support at +1-800-555-0147.

## Returns and refunds

### What is the return policy?

Most unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Final-sale items, gift cards and personalised products cannot be returned. Start a return from **Account > Orders > Return items**.

### Are returns free?

Returns are free when an item is defective, damaged or incorrect. For preference-based returns, a $4.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

### When will I receive my refund?

Northstar Shop issues the refund within 3 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Banks and card issuers may take another 5 to 10 business days to post the credit.

### Can I exchange an item?

Direct exchanges are not available. Return the eligible item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement.

## Payments

### Which payment methods are accepted?

Northstar Shop accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Google Pay. It does not accept cash, cheques, bank transfers or cryptocurrency.

### When is my card charged?

A temporary authorisation may appear when the order is placed. The final charge is captured when the order ships. Split shipments can produce separate charges whose total equals the order total.

### Why was my payment declined?

Verify the card number, expiry date, security code and billing address. Confirm that the card has enough available funds and is enabled for online purchases. If it is still declined, contact the card issuer or try another accepted payment method.

### How are refunds sent?

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Northstar Shop cannot redirect a refund to a different card or payment account.

## Products

### How can I tell whether a product is in stock?

Product pages show current availability. An item marked **Out of stock** cannot be ordered. Select **Notify me** on its product page to receive an email when it becomes available again.

### Does Northstar Shop price match other retailers?

Northstar Shop does not offer price matching. Prices and promotions can change without notice, but an order keeps the price displayed when checkout was completed.

## Accounts

### How do I reset my password?

Select **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to the account email address. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

### How do I update my email or saved address?

Open **Account > Profile** to update the account email and saved delivery addresses. Changes do not update an order that has already been placed.

### How do I close my account?

Call support at +1-800-555-0147 to request account closure. The support team must verify account ownership before processing the request.

## Privacy

### How does Northstar Shop use my data?

Northstar Shop uses account, order and device data to operate the shop, prevent fraud and provide customer support. It does not sell personal data.

### How can I request a copy or deletion of my personal data?

Call support at +1-800-555-0147. The support team verifies account ownership before accepting a data access or deletion request. Some transaction records may be retained where required by law.

## Human support

For issues that cannot be answered by this FAQ, call +1-800-555-0147. The fictional support line is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time.
